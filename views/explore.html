Naoko Yamada (山田尚子)

思春期の建築学、微細な身体所作、そして色彩の共感覚

From her epochal framing at Kyoto Animation to her fluid chromatic exploration with Science SARU in The Colors Within (きみの色), Yamada has redefined animation grammar. Her camera disavows spectacle, obsessing over the tremor of a collarbone, the cadence of shoes against polished linoleum, and the acoustic isolation of youthful longing.

“The physical weight of light falling across piano hammers...”
